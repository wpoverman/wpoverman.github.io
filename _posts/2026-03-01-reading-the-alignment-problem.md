Brian Christian's *The Alignment Problem* remains one of the best introductions to the field for a general audience. Re-reading it two years later, what strikes me is how much the landscape has shifted.

> Machine learning is, in its essence, a kind of averaging. And averages can be dangerous.

The chapter on reward hacking feels almost quaint now — the problems Christian describes have become far more concrete and urgent as models have scaled.

## Key takeaways

1. The alignment problem is not a single problem but a family of related concerns
2. Technical solutions are necessary but not sufficient
3. The social and institutional dimensions matter as much as the mathematical ones
