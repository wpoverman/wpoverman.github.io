William Overman

PhD, Stanford GSB

My research develops approaches to AI safety, focused on how humans can effectively oversee AI systems whose capabilities increasingly surpass their own.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Operations, Information, and Technology at Stanford Graduate School of Business, advised by Mohsen Bayati. Before Stanford, I was a visiting researcher at the Institute for Basic Science in South Korea and earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science from UC Irvine. I graduated from Caltech with a double major in Mathematics and Computer Science.
