I study how to make AI systems that are both safe and useful — calibrating the tension between autonomy and oversight using tools from conformal prediction, game theory, and causal inference.

Advised by Mohsen Bayati at Stanford. Previously at Illinois and UCI. My work has appeared at ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, and AISTATS. I'm interested in the question of how much freedom to give an AI agent, and how to answer that question rigorously.
